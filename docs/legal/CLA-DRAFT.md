# Individual Contributor License Agreement (Draft)

> **DRAFT — NOT ACTIVE**
>
> This document is a working draft and has **not** been approved as an active contributor agreement.
>
> **Pull requests may be opened for review, but external code must not be merged under this draft.**
>
> Legal review and identification of the receiving individual or legal entity are required before activation.
>
> Draft version: **0.1**  
> Status: **NOT ACTIVE**

Derived conceptually from established contributor agreement practices; final a11yst terms require independent legal review.

---

## 1. Definitions

**"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including modifications and additions to existing work, that is intentionally submitted by a Contributor for inclusion in the a11yst Community project.

**"Contributor"** means the individual who submits a Contribution, or the legal entity on whose behalf a Contribution is submitted when authorized.

**"Project"** or **"a11yst Community"** means the open-source software project distributed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0) in this repository.

**"Receiving Party"** means **[RECEIVING PARTY TO BE CONFIRMED BEFORE ACTIVATION]**.

---

## 2. Copyright ownership

The Contributor **retains ownership** of their Contribution.

This draft does **not** require copyright assignment as the default contribution model.

---

## 3. Copyright license — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

The final agreement should include a copyright license from the Contributor to the Receiving Party. Topics for counsel to define include whether the license should be:

- worldwide;
- non-exclusive;
- royalty-free;
- sufficiently durable or perpetual where legally appropriate;
- covering use, reproduction, modification, derivative works, distribution, sublicensing, and relicensing when expressly approved in the final legal text.

**Status:** placeholder only. No rights are granted by this draft.

---

## 4. Community distribution under MPL-2.0

Accepted Contributions merged into the a11yst Community repository are intended to be distributed as part of a11yst Community under **MPL-2.0**, unless a file clearly states another compatible licensing arrangement.

The current license of a11yst Community is MPL-2.0.

---

## 5. Commercial licensing objective — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

The final agreement should explicitly determine whether and to what extent the Receiving Party may include, relicense, sublicense, or otherwise use accepted Contributions in separately licensed commercial offerings while the Contributor retains ownership of the original Contribution.

This draft makes the project intent visible for legal review. It is **not** an active grant of commercial-use rights beyond what MPL-2.0 already provides for Community distribution.

---

## 6. Patent grant — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

Topics for counsel to consider:

- contributor patent claims necessarily infringed by the Contribution;
- scope of any patent license;
- termination or defensive termination;
- treatment of corporate contributors and employer-owned inventions.

**Status:** no patent grant is made by this draft.

---

## 7. Representations — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

The final agreement should require the Contributor to represent, subject to legal review, that:

- they have the right to submit the Contribution;
- the Contribution is their original work or they have sufficient rights to submit it;
- they identify third-party or externally sourced material included in the Contribution;
- the Contribution does not incorporate confidential information they are not authorized to disclose;
- if submitting on behalf of an employer or other entity, they have authority to do so;
- they will notify the project of relevant conflicts with employer IP obligations when known.

This draft does not impose binding representations.

---

## 8. Employer and third-party rights

Contributors may be subject to employment agreements, invention assignment policies, or other third-party rights. Contributors are responsible for determining whether additional authorization is required before submitting a Contribution.

See [docs/contributing-ip.md](../contributing-ip.md).

---

## 9. Moral rights — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

Treatment of moral rights, particularly across jurisdictions, must be determined with counsel. This draft does not include a moral-rights waiver.

---

## 10. Revocability / termination — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

Whether and how a contributor may revoke or terminate granted rights, and the effect on already-distributed Community releases, must be determined with counsel.

---

## 11. Governing law / venue — TO BE DETERMINED WITH COUNSEL

Governing law and dispute venue depend on the confirmed Receiving Party and applicable counsel guidance.

---

## 12. Contributor capacity / minors — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

Age and legal capacity requirements for contributors must be determined with counsel.

---

## 13. Warranty / indemnity scope — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

The scope of contributor representations, warranty disclaimers, and any indemnity provisions must be determined with counsel. This draft does not alter MPL-2.0 warranty disclaimers for distributed Community code.

---

## 14. Activation

This document becomes operative only after:

1. independent legal review;
2. confirmation of the Receiving Party;
3. publication of a final, active agreement at a non-draft location;
4. establishment of a signing and record-keeping workflow;
5. explicit update of contribution documentation to open external code merges.

See [CLA-REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md](./CLA-REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md).
