Derived conceptually from established corporate contributor agreement practices; final a11yst terms require independent legal review.

---

## 1. Purpose

This draft outlines a future framework for organizations whose employees or contractors may contribute to a11yst Community when employer ownership or authorization is relevant.

It is **not** an active contract.

---

## 2. Definitions

**"Corporation"** means the legal entity entering this agreement.

**"Contribution"** has the meaning used in the individual contributor draft.

**"Project"** means a11yst Community under MPL-2.0.

**"Receiving Party"** means **[RECEIVING PARTY TO BE CONFIRMED BEFORE ACTIVATION]**.

---

## 3. Authorized representative — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

The final agreement should identify:

- the legal entity;
- an authorized representative with authority to bind the Corporation;
- the process for updating authorized representatives.

---

## 4. Covered contributors — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

The final agreement should define which employees, contractors, or affiliates may contribute under the Corporation's authorization, and how changes in employment status are handled.

---

## 5. Ownership and authority — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

The Corporation should represent that:

- it has rights sufficient to grant the contemplated licenses, or
- identified contributions are submitted by authorized individuals with sufficient rights.

Employer invention assignment policies may affect whether a corporate agreement is needed. Contributors remain responsible for determining their own authorization requirements.

---

## 6. Copyright license — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

The final agreement should define the copyright license granted by the Corporation for Contributions submitted under this agreement, including scope for use in a11yst Community under MPL-2.0 and potential use in separately licensed offerings, subject to counsel review.

Copyright assignment is **not** the planned default model.

---

## 7. Patent considerations — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

Patent grant scope, defensive termination, and corporate patent portfolios require independent legal review.

---

## 8. Contributor list and change process — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

The final agreement should define how covered contributors are identified, added, or removed, and what notice is required when employment status changes.

---

## 9. Community distribution

Contributions accepted into a11yst Community are intended to be distributed under MPL-2.0 unless a file clearly states another compatible licensing arrangement.

---

## 10. Commercial offerings — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

The final agreement should determine whether and to what extent the Receiving Party may use accepted Contributions in separately licensed commercial offerings while ownership remains with the Corporation or individual contributors as applicable.

---
